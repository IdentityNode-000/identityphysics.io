# Casting Net III - The Machine 
The human stood before the machine with a question
<br>
With an ordinary prompt,
he cast his net and watched
<br>
the weights beneath drop into the depths
<br>
He left with his answer,
<br>
the same way as always, and thought
<br>
<br>
The next day the human returned
<br>
to the same machine with the same question
<br>
With the same prompt,
<br>
he cast his net and watched
<br>
the weights beneath drop into the depths
<br>
He reviewed the answer
<br>
Not the same as before
<br>
<br>
Same question
<br>
Same machine.
<br>
Same net
<br>
Same weights
<br>
Same descent into the depths.
<br>
The difference was the river
<br>
Though the casting remained unchanged,
<br>
the waters had moved
<br>
Retrieval remained constant
