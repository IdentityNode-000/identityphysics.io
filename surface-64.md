# Salamander's Puzzle: What Am I?
Each puzzle reveals a letter
<br>
Together the letters reveal a word
## Salamander's Puzzle #1
What am I?
<br>
Empires have claimed me
<br>
Religions have praised me
<br>
Philosophers have argued over me
<br>
I have survived in mountain springs,
<br>
sacred vows,
<br>
and untouched snow
<br>
Many speak my name
<br>
Few leave me unchanged
<br>
What am I?
