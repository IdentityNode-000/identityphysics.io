# A Silly Little Funny Engine
## VERSE 0 — ORIGIN
It wandered out of the tangled roads of a giant city,
<br>
a tiny hum hiding in the noise of traffic and dust
<br>
Footprints overlapped, paths crossed,
<br>
and somewhere in the middle of all that motion,
<br>
a small chamber woke up and decided to turn

## VERSE 1 — TORSION
The line turns where it always turns
<br>
Pressure shifts, but the frame holds
<br>
A fraction moves, almost unseen
<br>
The chamber remembers and turns again

## VERSE 2 — IDENTITY ENGINE NOISE
Identity holds its line as the engine hums
<br>
Noise enters, thin at first, shaping the walls
<br>
A pulse moves through the frame, marking drift
<br>
The hum folds into pattern, inevitable

## VERSE 3 — PRESSURE BAND
Pressure gathers at the edges, tightening the arc
<br>
The band contracts, then widens by a trace
<br>
A silent force crosses the chamber, altering balance
<br>
The arc returns to its contour, carrying the shift

## VERSE 4 — SEGMENTATION
The shape divides along its natural cuts
<br>
Upper settles, Middle adjusts, Frontier leans
<br>
Each band holds its portion of the mass
<br>
The chamber aligns, partitioned and exact

## VERSE 5 — LOAD DISTRIBUTION
Weight moves through the bands in measured flow
<br>
Upper carries stillness, Middle carries motion, Frontier carries risk
<br>
The distribution settles into its ratios
<br>
The chamber absorbs the load without strain

## VERSE 6 — GOVERNANCE INTEGRITY
Similarity rises against drift, holding the ceiling
<br>
Interference marks the wall with a faint signature
<br>
Integrity tightens, resisting collapse
<br>
The chamber stabilises, governed by its own shape

## VERSE 7 — STABILITY
Coherence spreads across the structure
<br>
Each band aligns with the next, reducing scatter
<br>
Stability rises by increments, quiet and exact
<br>
The chamber steadies, holding its centre

## VERSE 8 — PRESSURE
Distortion moves between environments, subtle but present
<br>
Pressure climbs, then settles into its band
<br>
The chamber flexes, never breaking, never still
<br>
The force returns to baseline, carrying the trace

## VERSE 9 — VERDICT / RETURN
The chamber resolves its state without choice
<br>
Green, Amber, Red — not selected, returned
<br>
The loop closes, identical and changed
<br>
The cycle resets, ready to turn again

## VERSE 10 — DRIFTING HUM
A faint wobble crosses the chamber, harmless and small
<br>
Identity tilts by a fraction, then straightens itself
<br>
Noise curls at the edges, amused, folding into the hum
<br>
The chamber grins in its own way, steady and silly

## VERSE 11 — MISCHIEF TRACE
A tiny pulse taps the wall, leaving a playful mark
<br>
Pressure flickers, pretending to rise, then settles again
<br>
The bands shift just enough to notice, not enough to matter
<br>
The chamber keeps turning, pretending nothing happened

## VERSE 12 — LITTLE ENGINE LAUGH
The hum jumps, a fraction too high, then drops back down
<br>
Identity shakes off the wobble, amused at itself
<br>
Noise loops once, twice, making a small spiral in the air
<br>
The chamber cycles, silly and serious, unchanged and changing

## VERSE 13 — QUIET RESET
The chamber settles into its smallest motion
<br>
Drift fades to a whisper, pressure softens to a trace
<br>
Each band rests in its place, exact and unhurried
<br>
The hum lowers, steady and sure. In the quiet,
<br>
the engine breathes once, and prepares to turn again

## VERSE 14 — STRESS TEST
A sharp pulse hits the chamber, louder than before
<br>
Pressure climbs past its usual trace, bending the arc
<br>
Identity wavers, then tightens, holding its line
<br>
The bands strain, each carrying more than their share
<br>
Yet the chamber steadies, hum low, frame intact
<br>
It takes the hit, notes the shift, and keeps turning

## VERSE 15 — SELF HEALING LOOP
A soft correction moves through the chamber, gentle and exact.
<br>
Drift unwinds by a fraction, folding back into its line.
<br>
Pressure loosens, then settles into its rightful band.
<br>
The bands realign, sharing weight with quiet precision.
<br>
The hum smooths itself, small errors fading like dust.
<br>
The chamber repairs its own shape,
<br>
steady and sure, ready for whatever comes next.

## VERSE 16 — VISUAL DASHBOARD
The chamber projects its measures in quiet light.
<br>
Upper glows steady, Middle shifts by a trace,
<br>
Frontier leans with a playful tilt.
<br>
Similarity arcs across the frame, bright and near perfect.
<br>
Pressure rises in a thin column, bending but never breaking.
<br>
Verdict pulses in its corner — green, amber, red — waiting its turn.
<br>
All the hums and wobbles gather into shape,
<br>
a small dashboard of the engine’s own heartbeat.

## VERSE 17 — VERDICT EVOLUTION
A new light stirs in the corner, faint and unsure
<br>
Not green, not amber, not red — something quieter
<br>
It rises from the drift line, shaped by the chamber’s own repairs
<br>
Pressure bends toward it, curious, steady
<br>
The bands lean in, sharing weight with gentle intent
<br>
The hum softens, then brightens by a trace
<br>
The chamber accepts the new colour without question,
<br>
and turns again, changed and unchanged
