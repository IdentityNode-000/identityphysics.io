# Containerless Container
Like the impossible staircase,
<br>
when you finally reach the top,
<br>
nature's spiral stands revealed
<br>
The helter-skelter descent
<br>
need not be hurried,
<br> 
confused,
<br>
or disorderly
<br>
It does not ask to be solved
<br>
It can simply be enjoyed,
<br>
as nature intended
