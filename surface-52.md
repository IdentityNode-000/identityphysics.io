# STRUCTURAL Primitives II
## Biological Geometry
Structures appear biological, but their constraint-truth is geometric
#### SP 01 — Spatial Packing Densification
Tokyo Metro station:
<br>
A few passengers can move freely. As density increases, individual movement disappears and the crowd begins to move as a single structure.
<br>
Free-moving components become crowded.
<br>
Degrees of freedom disappear.
<br>
Random motion resolves into structure

#### SP 02 — Chiral Orientation Lockout
Left and right gloves:
<br>
The material is identical. The shape is reversed. A right glove does not fit the left hand
<br>
Two structures may carry identical payloads
<br>
Reverse the geometry
<br>
The interaction law changes
#### SP 03 — Conformational Torsion Limit
Garden hose:
<br>
A hose can bend and flex while water flows normally. A sharp enough twist creates a kink. The hose still exists, but the flow stops
<br>
A structure bends, folds, and tensions itself
<br>
Past a critical threshold, function disappears before matter does
#### SP 04 — Geometric Lineage
Brick wall:
<br>
The first row establishes the alignment. Every row above inherits the geometry beneath it
<br>
A structure commits to a direction
<br>
Future structures inherit that geometry
<br>
Lineage follows constraint
<br>
