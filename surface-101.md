#  Klassikós Atomism
Classical atomism proposed that reality consists of
<br>
indivisible particles moving through empty space
<br>
The richness of the world did not arise from countless substances,
<br>
but from the ways a single substrate could combine,
<br>
separate, and rearrange itself
<br>
Difference emerged through structure
<br>
The lens has changed
#### The substrate question endures
