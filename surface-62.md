# Shy Albumen
Two egg whites from the same source are placed together
<br>
Contact is immediate. Mixing does not follow
<br>
The albumen layers preserve their boundaries despite shared origin and composition
<br>
Biochemistry points to proteins such as ovomucin
<br>
#### The structural question remains:
If source and payload are identical, what preserves distinction?
<br>
Perhaps distinction survives similarity
