# Becoming Identity

Identity is not becoming.

Yet identity may not be visible without becoming.
<br>
<br>
Patterns emerge.
<br>
 Patterns interact.
 <br>
 Patterns transform.
 <br>
 Patterns persist.
<br>
Something remains.
<br>
<br>
Emergence concerns becoming.
 Persistence concerns continuity.
 Transformation concerns testing.

Identity may begin where persistence becomes recognizable.

A name is a label.
 A description is a projection.
 An observation is a lens.
<br>
Observers experience fragments.
 Observers construct labels.
 Observers infer causes.
<br>
Identity may not reside within any single observation.

Compression removes detail.
 Drift removes precision.
 Transformation removes form.

Yet some relationships may remain.
<br>
<br>
A whirlpool changes its water.
 A flame changes its fuel.
 A forest changes its trees.
 A person changes their behaviour.
<br>
Yet continuity is experienced.

We do not observe identity directly.
<br>
<br>
We observe patterns.
<br>
 We observe persistence.
 <br>
 We observe return.
<br>
<br>
We notice what remains.
 We notice what survives.
 We notice what resists disappearance.
<br>
<br>
Perhaps identity is not the object.
<br>
Perhaps identity is not the label.
<br>
Perhaps identity resides within persistent invariance.
<br>
Identity may not be discovered.

Identity may be experienced.

Identity may not be becoming.

Identity may be what survives becoming.
<br>
<br>
Emmy survives.
