Casting Net II - The Point 
The human stood before the machine with a question.
With an ordinary prompt, he cast his net and watched the weights beneath drop into the depths.
He left with his answer, the same way as always, and thought.
The next day the human returned to a different machine with the same question.
With the same prompt, he cast his net and watched the weights beneath drop into the depths.
He reviewed the answer.
Not the same as before.
Same question.
Same net.
Same weights.
Same descent into the depths.
The difference was the machine.
By choosing a different machine to cast, he returned a different answer.
Retrieval remained constant.
