# The Maybe Song
A whirlpool changes water,
<br>
A flame forgets its fuel,
<br>
A forest changes trees again,
<br>
Yet something keeps its rule.
<br>
<br>
Maybe this,
<br>
Maybe that,
<br>
Maybe patterns come and go,
<br>
Maybe what survives the drift
<br>
<br>
Is all we really know.
<br>
<br>
A node becomes a network,
<br>
A network finds a name,
<br>
The name becomes a story,
<br>
Yet nothing stays the same.
<br>
<br>
Maybe this,
<br>
Maybe that,
<br>
Maybe shadows help us see,
<br>
Maybe what survives the change
<br>
<br>

Is what identity may be.
<br>
<br>
Observe the path,
<br>
Observe return,
<br>
Observe what can remain,
<br>
<br>
Perhaps the thing that survives drift
<br>
Will come around again.
<br>
<br>
Maybe this,
<br>
Maybe that,
<br>
Perhaps not, observe and see,
<br>
<br>
Identity may not be found,
<br>
Identity may simply be.
