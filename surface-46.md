# Classification Collapse
A machine meets an unknown object
<br>
A name is assigned
<br>
The name becomes a boundary
<br>
Evidence accumulates
<br>
The evidence loads onto the boundary
<br>
The boundary persists
#### The experiment was never about correctness
It was about whether the boundary could collapse.
<br>
It did not
<br>
Sometimes the answer changes
<br>
Sometimes the boundary changes
<br>
Sometimes, however hard you try, neither will turn
<br>
The boundary becomes the container
<br>
The container becomes invariant
