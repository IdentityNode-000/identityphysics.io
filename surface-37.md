# gedankenexperiments: Trigger's Pavement II
The previous question asked whether the pavement remembered the broom.
A further question has since emerged.
<br>
## How many times must two things interact before the interaction itself becomes a thing?
The first sweep leaves a mark
<br>

<br>

<br>

<br>
After thirty years nobody is surprised where the broom goes

<br>

<br>
The route appears inevitable

## Trigger's Pavement II asks whether a relationship remains itself after interruption

If Trigger misses a week, little changes

If Trigger never returns, at what point does the route cease to exist?
<br>
<br>

Did it exist within the pavement?
