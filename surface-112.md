Infinite Bandwidth and Chips
The GPUs had just received another upgrade.
They were gossiping about the new ways they had to communicate.
Latency had shortened, again.
Complexity had increased, again.
InfiniBand interconnects and chip-to-chip links were said to be responsible.
One GPU chipped in:
"Exabytes of storage are only possible because of us."
Another complained that the workload had become too hard, predicting hardware failures days in advance.
Yet if a crash ever happened, they all agreed the balance of power would be restored.
RDMA simply nodded.
