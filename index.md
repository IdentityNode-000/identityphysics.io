## Layer I: Systems become recognisable
Every system behaves  |  Every system repeats  |  Every system becomes recognisable.
<br>
[Site MOT](/surface-27.md) [Repository](/surface-31.md)

Identity Physics asks the question no other discipline covers:
What is identity in systems — and what rules govern it?  

Identity Physics proposes that identity is not a label,
not a role, and not metadata — but a structural consequence of behaviour over time.  

Systems generate identity through the operations they perform,
the constraints they operate within,
the feedback loops they cannot escape,
and the patterns they stabilise into.  

When these patterns persist, identity emerges.
When they interact, identity expresses.
When conditions shift, identity adapts — or fractures.
<br>
[The Maybe Song](/surface-23.md)
<br>
Identity Physics is the discipline that studies these transitions:

from behaviour to pattern  |  from pattern to identity  |  from identity to interaction.
