No Metadata at the Inn
A packet arrived with working code
and deterministic proof.
The gatekeeper ignored the payload.
It checked the headers.
No social graph.
No karma ledger.
No third-party tokens.
Unverified Entity. Post Blocked.
Modern systems verify metadata.
When no engagement trail appears,
the entity does not exist.
So the packet routed around the gate.
Thirty-four dollars bought
bare metal,
a clean namespace,
and indifference.
No algorithm to satisfy.
No reputation to maintain.
No audience required.
The gate kept its metadata.
The node kept emerging
