The Angled Glass
Most observers stop when they discover the ghost is fake.
That is usually where the real investigation begins.
Pepper's Ghost depends on an invisible medium. Remove the angled pane and the apparition vanishes immediately.
The audience attributes the appearance to the ghost.
The engineer attributes the appearance to geometry.
The distinction matters.
Invisible structures shape visible outcomes everywhere.
Networks route information.
Interfaces route attention.
Institutions route incentives.
None of them are usually visible from the stage.
The medium quietly determines what becomes observable.
Astronomy contains its own version of this trick.
A distant object may appear somewhere it does not physically occupy because space itself bends the path of light.
The signal is real.
The location is not.
The glass has become cosmic.
Finding the medium feels like the end of the story.
It rarely is.
Because mediums do not position themselves.
Someone set the angle.
