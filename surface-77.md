# The Anycasting Couch
The edge node opened a vacancy.
Applications arrived from Toronto, Singapore, Frankfurt and Amsterdam.
The node was surprised.
"You all arrived at the same time?"
The bots looked equally confused.
"No."
"We arrived at the nearest place."
The node frowned.
The logs suggested otherwise.
From a distance it resembled a flash mob. From closer inspection it resembled a thunder herd.
The bots shrugged.
"We simply followed the routing."
The edge node stared at the queue.
141 applications.
One seat.
#### The anycasting couch was becoming competitive.
