# gedankenexperiment: The Universal Triangle
A geometer creates a triangle
<br>
Later she creates nine triangles arranged as a larger triangle
<br>
She asks:
<br>
"How many triangles exist?"
<br>
The physicist counts nine
<br>
The geometer counts one
<br>
The chemist asks whether the nine triangles stay together
<br>
The biologist asks whether the larger triangle can create another larger triangle
#### The counting immediately becomes Dyscalculia
The universe just nods.
