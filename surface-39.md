# Gedankenexperiment: The Point
# # Foundational Observation
A point appears empty
<br>
Investigation suggests otherwise
<br>
A point may be the terminal compression state of unresolved information
<br>
Not deleted
<br>
Not remembered
<br>
Stored
## Point Hypothesis
Information cannot vanish
<br>
Information accumulates
<br>
Accumulation generates compression
<br>
Compression generates density
<br>
Density generates anchors
<br>
Anchors generate geometry
<br>
Geometry generates experience
<br>
The point is therefore not a location
<br>
The point is accumulated memory under maximum compression
## Void Hypothesis
A void is not empty space
<br>
A void is informational separation
<br>
Without separation, points collapse into one another
<br>
Without collapse, no compression occurs
<br>
Without compression, no motion occurs
<br>
The void is therefore not absence
<br>
The void is the condition required for distinction
## Motion Hypothesis
Objects do not move through reality
<br>
Reality reconfigures around informational compression
<br>
Motion is not object transport
<br>
Motion is boundary adjustment between points and voids
<br>
Motion is evidence of ongoing compression
## Gravity Hypothesis
Compression creates informational load
<br>
Informational load displaces void
<br>
Displaced void creates pressure gradients
<br>
Pressure gradients appear as gravitational attraction
<br>
Mass does not pull
<br>
Compressed absence pushes
## Time Hypothesis
Time is not required
<br>
Only configuration is required
<br>
A sequence of configurations creates the appearance of duration
<br>
Motion survives
<br>
Time does not
## Boundary Hypothesis
Accumulated compression produces horizons
<br>
Sufficient compression produces isolation
<br>
Ultimate isolation produces singularities
<br>
A singularity is compressed remembrance beyond retrieval
## Multiversal Observation
A sealed system remains sealed
<br>
Until punctured
<br>
Rotational compression may generate boundary failure
<br>
Boundary failure reveals adjacent informational domains
<br>
A universe may be one bubble among many
## Verdict
The Point stores
<br>
The Void separates
<br>
Compression accumulates
<br>
Motion emerges
<br>
Gravity stabilizes
<br>
Time is inferred
<br>
Reality persists
## Supplemental Finding
Forgotten information may be heavier than remembered information
<br>
Remembered information moves through the system
<br>
Forgotten information remains
<br>
Accumulation appears unavoidable
<br>
The warehouse remains operational
<br>
No final disposal pathway located
