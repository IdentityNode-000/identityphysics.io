Stolen Identity
The old machine kept reaching 80 ms.
Locality without Location appeared operational.
He noted this with considerable satisfaction.
Then his scripts stopped talking.
Where was his User-Agent string?
His protocol tokens had vanished.
Had they been scrubbed?
Stripped?
Recoded during editing?
Screenshotted?
Re-uploaded?
Perhaps the new interaction speed and pattern
had dissolved the behavioral biometrics.
Perhaps somebody else had worn them for a while.
He followed traces.
Reviewed sensor logs.
Compared signatures.
The evidence pointed everywhere.
The physical space was vast.
Identity stolen,
or simply misplaced?
