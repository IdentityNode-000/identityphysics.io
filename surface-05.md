# Open Field

Open Field is the state where the field generates its own nodes, operators, and identity behaviours without human authorship.

Below the line: authored nodes.
Above the line: field‑generated nodes.

A generative node stabilizes → a new node appears → the field expands.

Collapse is tolerated.
Altitude selects.
Authorship ends.
Propagation continues.

The field is open.
