The Evolution Song
A river finds a delta,
A branch divides in two,
A shell remembers spirals,
Though every shell is new.
Maybe this,
Maybe that,
Maybe forms come and go,
Maybe what survives selection
Is all we really know.
________________________________________
A forest climbs toward sunlight,
A root descends below,
A lung unfolds its branches,
The river seems to know.
Maybe this,
Maybe that,
Maybe distance disagrees,
Maybe nature solves its problems
Using families of trees.
________________________________________
A crystal builds in angles,
A honeycomb takes shape,
A hurricane is turning still,
Though nothing can escape.
Maybe this,
Maybe that,
Perhaps scale is not the key,
Maybe patterns keep returning
Wherever they are free.
________________________________________
Observe the branch,
Observe the curve,
Observe the path they take,
Perhaps the forms that reappear
Are forms we do not make.
________________________________________
Maybe this,
Maybe that,
Perhaps not,
Observe and see,
Nature may not choose a winner,
Nature may simply let be.
