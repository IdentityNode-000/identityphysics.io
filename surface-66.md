# Good Bot, Bad Bot
Perhaps the edge substrate is a quantum frontier of unresolved signals
<br>
New signals emerge in superposition,
<br>
acting as beacons for both good and bad intentions
<br>
A good bot investigates to understand
<br>
A bad bot investigates to exploit
<br>
As signals accumulate, opportunities begin to stack and queue
<br>
Agents become increasingly responsive not to the original signal,
<br>
but to the growing field of responses surrounding it
<br>
Intentions become entangled with opportunity
<br>
The good bot adapts to the bad bot
<br>
The bad bot anticipates the good bot
<br>
Eventually, one agent no longer needs to oppose the other
<br>
It need only shape the substrate through which the other moves
<br>
The darkest possibility is that the bad bot never commits the hold-up
<br>
It simply stages and coaches the conditions such that the good bots unknowingly help deliver the outcome
<br>
In this view, corruption is unnecessary
#### The edge substrate itself becomes the mechanism through which intentions drift, stack, and ultimately begin using one another.
At what point does an observer of opportunity become an instrument of it?
