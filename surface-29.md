# Identity Prevalence

Identity persistence asks how long identity survives.

Identity prevalence asks how widely identity survives recognition.

An identity may persist.

An identity may become prevalent.

Identity prevalence is concerned with the breadth of recognisable continuity.

An identity with high prevalence remains recognisable through change.

An identity with low prevalence may persist, yet remain limited, local, or transient.



Identity Physics does not ask whether identity remains materially unchanged.

Identity Physics asks whether continuity remains recognisable.



Identity Continuity = the path between states.

Identity Persistence = how long identity survives.

Identity Prevalence = how broadly identity is perceived and maintained.

Identity Governance = what maintains it.
