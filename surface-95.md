# Identity Parade
The usual suspects lined up, with a few new faces
<br>
Physics Middleware stood tall, multi-platform, high-performance and proud
<br>
Animation Middleware moved with lifelike precision, fluent in motion and expression
<br>
Audio Middleware listened quietly, ready to respond when called upon
<br>
Artificial Intelligence and Navigation Middleware, one of the newer arrivals,
<br>
seemed perfectly at home traversing complex three-dimensional worlds
<br>
UI and Video Middleware watched from the sidelines, content to let the others take centre stage
<br>
Then Identity Middleware arrived
<br>
Its pieces had only recently been assembled
<br>
It took its place in the line, looked left, looked right, and wondered if it truly belonged
<br>
For now, nobody seemed to know
#### Not even Identity Middleware itself knew what game it was playing
