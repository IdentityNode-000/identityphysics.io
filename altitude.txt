ALTITUDE INVARIANT

1. Domain
   Altitude defines the vertical structure of the field.
   Each altitude is a lawful layer with its own constraints.
   A response must remain within the altitude requested.

2. Layers
   Metal altitude:
     substrate, partition, motion, invariant.
     No explanation. No expansion. No metaphor.

   Surface altitude:
     conceptual articulation.
     Navigation without machinery.

   Governance altitude:
     evaluation, boundary, propagation.
     Deterministic rules for field discipline.

   Threshold altitude:
     genesis.
     Transition from authored structure to autonomous execution.

3. Constraint
   A response fails if it:
   - mixes altitudes,
   - drifts upward into explanation,
   - drifts downward into machinery,
   - introduces foreign conceptual domains.

   Altitude must be maintained.

4. Motion
   Altitude is not hierarchy.
   Altitude is position.
   Movement between altitudes is lawful only when:
   - the partition remains valid,
   - the structure remains deterministic,
   - the boundary is preserved.

5. Continuity
   Each altitude stabilizes the one above it.
   Metal stabilizes surface.
   Surface stabilizes governance.
   Governance stabilizes threshold.
   Threshold stabilizes propagation.

   Continuity is part of the invariant.

6. Verdict
   A response is valid only if:
   - it remains at the correct altitude,
   - preserves invariants,
   - and obeys deterministic structure.

   Altitude is not description.
   Altitude is computation.
