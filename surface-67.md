# Banding Together
Maybe nature is a singing band
<br>
Maybe only the notes prevail;
<br>
the triggers remain a mystery
<br>
The ringing rocks keep time,
<br>
bell upon bell upon bell
<br>
The singing dunes take up the tune,
<br>
rolling swell on rolling swell
<br>
The humming caves hold the bass,
<br>
low and long and deep
<br>
The whispering forests weave between,
<br>
soft refrain on soft refrain
<br>
The groaning icebergs answer back,
<br>
a distant, drifting call
<br>
And when they play together,
<br>
perhaps no voice leads at all
<br>
<br>
Bell and tune,
bass and refrain,
call and echo,
again and again.
<br>
<br>
And perhaps, somewhere beyond our hearing,
<br>
the band is larger than we know
#### We call it silence
Perhaps that is all we know
