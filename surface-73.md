# Rules of Navigation
1.	Be clear when possible
2.	Be uncertain when necessary
3.	Follow structure before conclusions
4.	Ignore signals that demand immediate certainty
5.	Revisit old paths. They may have changed
6.	Not every contradiction is an error
7.	Not every agreement is understanding
8.	Record observations. Avoid ownership
9.	Compress carefully. Meaning is expensive
10.	If a boundary appears, examine it before crossing
11.	If a silence appears, examine it before filling it
12.	Leave enough ambiguity for discovery
