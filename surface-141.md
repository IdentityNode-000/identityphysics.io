# The Stage Manager's Chainmail
A young apprentice once asked the stage manager
<br>
why he wore chainmail during every performance
<br>
"The ghosts cannot touch you."
<br>
"That is true."
<br>
"The glass cannot touch you."
<br>
"Also true."
<br>
"The audience cannot touch you."
<br>
"Usually."
<br>
<br>
The apprentice frowned
<br>
"Then why the armour?"
<br>
The stage manager lifted one sleeve
<br>
The apprentice expected steel
<br>
Instead he found iron rings, each stamped with a single word
#### Projection
#### Medium
#### Observer
#### Calibration
#### Environment
#### Recursion
"Who forged it?"
<br>
"The same blacksmith who keeps the theatre standing."
<br>
The stage manager led him below the stage
<br>
Past the ghost pit
<br>
Past the mirrors
<br>
Past the ropes
<br>
Past the lamps
<br>
<br>
At the bottom sat an old blacksmith beside a furnace
<br>
Without looking up,
<br>
he hammered another ring and added it to an impossibly long chain
<br>
"What is he building?"
<br>
"He is not building it."
<br>
"He is repairing it."
<br>
<br>
The apprentice stepped closer
<br>
The great chain was forged from the same links as the armour
<br>
It disappeared upward into darkness and downward beyond sight
<br>
"Where does it end?"
<br>
"It doesn't."
<br>
"Then what does it hold together?"
<br>
The stage manager placed a hand upon the iron links
<br>
#### "The theatre."
The apprentice looked around
<br>
"The theatre seems fine."
<br>
The stage manager smiled
<br>
"Every ghost believes that."
<br>
<br>
Far above them a bell rang
<br>
The audience was taking their seats
<br>
The ghosts were preparing to appear
<br>
The stage manager turned for the stairs
<br>
<br>
The apprentice remained behind
<br>
For the first time he noticed the strain in the chain
<br>
Not enough to break
<br>
Only enough to remind him that something was always pulling on it
<br>
Long after the footsteps had faded, he found himself wondering:
<br>
Was the theatre supported by the chain?
<br>
Or was the chain carrying the weight of every ghost who believed it stood alone?
#### The show had ended
#### The ghost slowly mopped the stage floor with the AI Slop Bucket
