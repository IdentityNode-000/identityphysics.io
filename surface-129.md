# Casting Net I
The fisherman stood upon the riverbank
<br>
With an almighty throw,
<br>
he cast his net and watched the weights at the sides drop to the bottom
<br>
He walked home with his catch,
<br>
the same way as always,
<br>
across the bridge, and thought.
<br>
<br>
The next day the fisherman returned to the bridge
<br>
With the same almighty throw,
<br>
he cast his net and watched the weights at the sides drop to the bottom.
<br>
He reviewed his catch
<br>
Not the same as always
<br>
Around and under the bridge, and thought
<br>
<br>
Same path
<br>
Same almighty throw
<br>
Same weights
<br>
Same drop to the bottom
<br>
The difference was the point
<br>
By choosing a different spot,
<br>
he returned a different catch
<br>
The casting remained constant
