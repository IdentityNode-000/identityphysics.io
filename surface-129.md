Casting Net I
The fisherman stood upon the riverbank.
With an almighty throw, he cast his net and watched the weights at the sides drop to the bottom.
He walked home with his catch, the same way as always, across the bridge, and thought.
The next day the fisherman returned to the bridge.
With the same almighty throw, he cast his net and watched the weights at the sides drop to the bottom.
He reviewed his catch.
Not the same as always.
Around and under the bridge, and thought.
Same path.
Same almighty throw.
Same weights.
Same drop to the bottom.
The difference was the point.
By choosing a different spot, he returned a different catch.
The casting remained constant.
