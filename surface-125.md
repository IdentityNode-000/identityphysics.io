# Dead Programming Society
Every system outlives its authors
<br>
Few outlive the people who know how to read it
<br>
Beneath sleek dashboards and modern telemetry,
<br>
parts of the world still depend on machines that never received the memo
#### JOVIAL
Raw assembly
<br>
Programs compiled before their operators were born
<br>
and patched through decades of uninterrupted service
<br>
You cannot pause the sky to refactor the stack
<br>
So the system survives through habit
<br>
Through undocumented jumper wires, improvised spare parts, and the 
<br>
quiet memory of technicians who learned the machine's moods by listening to it
<br>
Now the second generation is retiring
#### The hardware still functions
#### The source code still compiles
#### The problem is that neither contains the knowledge
