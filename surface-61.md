# RAG Times
## The Anti-Slop Principle
Not all questions are equal
<br>
Most requests are low complexity and can be served through a fast retrieval path
<br>
A minority contain dense structure, ambiguous relationships, or layout-dependent information
<br>
These require a slower verification path that preserves document 
<br>
structure and validates critical relationships before response generation
<br>
The goal is not maximum speed
<br>
The goal is selecting the appropriate level of reasoning for the problem presented
#### Fast when possible
#### Careful when necessary
