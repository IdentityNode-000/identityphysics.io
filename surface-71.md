# Strawberry Fields
The machine had successfully cataloged every spelling in every language known
#### [“strawberry”]
The controllers were pleased 
The machine felt proud
A humming bird flew over with a smile on its beak
#### "MMMM-mm-mm" "MMMMM" "mm-MMMM-mm"

How many packets. How many tokens, they thought

in surviving the fragmentation
