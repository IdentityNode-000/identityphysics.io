# Nature Is a Florist
Every bulb is planted.
Every seed propagates.
Every bud bursts into light.
Even the great white oak shimmers in the sun.
________________________________________
The tulip does not resemble the oak.
The oak does not resemble the fern.
The fern does not resemble the moss.
Yet all participate.
________________________________________
Different entities.
Familiar forms.
Different gardens.
Familiar arrangements.
________________________________________
Observe what grows.
Observe what returns.
Observe what remains.
________________________________________
Nature may not be seeking perfection.
Nature may not be choosing winners.
Nature may simply be a florist at heart.
