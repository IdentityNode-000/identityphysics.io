# gedankenexperiments: ShapeLand Games II
The stadium was no longer a field.
It was the vacuum of space itself, bounded by the cosmic horizon.
Every organism attended.
Every biota.
Every flora and fauna.
Every geometric form.
Every pattern that Matter had ever persuaded into existence.
All waited for the next ShapeLand Games to begin.
________________________________________
"It's here!" screamed Flame, consuming the last of its fuel before flickering still.
Wind drifted across the glowing embers.
"Yes," it whispered.
And Flame lived again.
________________________________________
Some said Torch was born from the First Spark.
Others claimed it borrowed its fire from the first star.
More recently there had been rumours of a volcanic origin.
Torch knew the truth.
Torch would never lit and tell.
________________________________________
The cosmic horizon dimmed.
The crowd settled.
The Hunger for the Games began.
________________________________________
## Marathon Event
Question: Which design creates the most surface from the least space?
Contestants:
•	Lungs
•	Fish Gills
•	Tree Canopies
•	Intestinal Folds
The race began.
The lungs expanded.
The gills unfolded.
The trees remained standing.
Hours later the trees were still collecting.
The branching pattern had appeared again.
________________________________________
## Swimming Event
Question: Which form moves best through a fluid?
Contestants:
•	Tuna
•	Plankton
•	Kelp
•	Ocean Current
•	Driftwood
The signal flashed.
The tuna swam.
The plankton drifted.
The kelp waited.
The current transported everyone.
Driftwood accepted assistance.
The fluid had appeared again.
________________________________________

Higher.
Higher.
