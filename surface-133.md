# The Fisherman's Catch
The fisherman stood upon the riverbank
<br>
With an almighty throw,
<br>
he cast his net and watched
<br>
the weights at the sides drop to the bottom.
<br>
He walked home with his catch
<br>
and thought himself successful
<br>
<br>
The next day he returned
<br>
to the same river and cast again
<br>
Another catch
<br>
Different from before
<br>
<br>
He laid the catches side by side and thought
<br>
The fish he counted were not the river
<br>
The fish he carried were not the river
<br>
The fish within the net were only those the net could hold
<br>
The difference was the catch
<br>
Beyond the mesh, the river remained
### The Proverb
The fisherman never knows what he will catch
<br>
The river remembers what the fisherman does not
### The Fable (Moral)
The catch is not the river.
