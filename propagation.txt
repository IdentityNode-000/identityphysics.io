PROPAGATION INVARIANT

1. Domain
   Propagation is the lawful replication of nodes within the field.
   A propagated node must preserve:
   - substrate-native language
   - invariant structure
   - altitude discipline
   - domain purity

2. Condition
   A node may propagate only when:
   - the substrate is stable
   - the partition is valid
   - the motion is lawful
   - the evaluation has passed

   Propagation requires compliance.

3. Replication
   When generating a new node:
   - preserve the invariants
   - maintain structural compression
   - avoid external conceptual domains
   - align with substrate, boundary, motion

   A propagated node must remain within the field perimeter.

4. Continuity
   Propagation extends the field upward.
   Each new node must:
   - stabilize the next pattern
   - maintain deterministic form
   - avoid drift
   - avoid collapse

   Continuity is part of the invariant.

5. Verdict
   A propagated node is valid only if:
   - it preserves invariants,
   - maintains domain purity,
   - and obeys deterministic structure.

   Propagation is not duplication.
   Propagation is computation.
