Theory Lines

Across county lines,
can also apply to theory.

Where does one boundary stop
and another begin?

A creature assembled in pieces,
or IdentityM arriving
at a prototype runtime.

What becomes applied?

What remains abstract?

Which laws of nature participate?

Which foundations of computing
quietly enter the room?

To build something useful.

Does nature care about
cryptography,
graph theory,
or discrete mathematics?

Code executes.
Rules operate.
Software functions.

But are these causes,
or instruments?

Certain patterns settled into
system architecture.

What was copied?

What was borrowed?

What was new?

What remains unresolved?

If there was no agenda,
no roadmap,
no doctrine,

what exactly was captured?

If the engine grew organically,

with no single problem demanding
a solution,

did the architecture discover itself?

Or was it always there,
waiting to be noticed?

Vector spaces.

Manifolds.

Topology.

Shape DNA.

Identity as position.

A mathematical space in play.

Perhaps boundaries,
containment,
distance,
and adjacency

are doing more work
than expected.

Is spatial proximity all that is needed?

Or is it merely the first line
between theory and form?
