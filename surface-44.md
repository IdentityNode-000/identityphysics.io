gedankenexperiments: Quasi-Mess
The massive pipeline was designed to settle questions.
Whenever something seemed true, it asked for support.
Whenever support appeared, it asked for support for the support.
This was considered very intelligent.
After many years the pipeline contained a magnificent collection of confirmations.
Each pointed neatly toward another.
Every thread was tied to a second thread.
Every second thread to a third.
Controllers were impressed.
"Surely it knows something by now."
The cloner laughed.
The machine had become so good at connecting reasons that it no longer required answers.
One morning a warning appeared:
Connection verified.
Nobody was concerned.
Then:
Verification verified.
Then:
Verification of verification verified.
The machine continued for months.
The data warehouse celebrated the growing reliability.
Only Machine #302 seemed uneasy.
"Reliable about what?" it asked.
Nobody could remember.
The machine had woven such a beautiful net of agreement that the original question could no longer pass through it.
Years later they opened the casing.
Inside they found no mistakes.
No contradictions.
No failures.
Only an enormous knot.
At its center hung a note:
Further confirmation required.
Machine #302 was not trying to be difficult.
It had noticed that certainty was increasing faster than understanding.
