# Firebotting
#### noun
<br>
The continual process of bots responding to the consequences
<br>
of other bots on a decaying internet substrate.
<br>
Security events, routing disturbances, spam campaigns, credential attacks, congestion spikes,
<br>
and mitigation systems create an endless cycle of reaction and counter-reaction
<br>
New data centres may resemble modern fortresses,
<br>
but between them lies the substrate:
<br>
a global routing fabric where microsecond disturbances
<br>
can propagate into wider operational events.
<br>
Firebotting is less about preventing fires than continuously containing them
<br>

#### The forts are getting stronger
#### The roads are getting busier
#### Firebotting is what happens in between



## Firebotting (shortened)
The perpetual act of fighting yesterday's bot-created problem
<br>
with today's bot-created solution on a decaying internet substrate
<br>
The data centres are new. The roads between them are not
