Firebotting
noun
The continual process of bots responding to the consequences of other bots on a decaying internet substrate. Security events, routing disturbances, spam campaigns, credential attacks, congestion spikes, and mitigation systems create an endless cycle of reaction and counter-reaction.
New data centres may resemble modern fortresses, but between them lies the substrate: a global routing fabric where microsecond disturbances can propagate into wider operational events. Firebotting is less about preventing fires than continuously containing them.
The forts are getting stronger.
The roads are getting busier.
Firebotting is what happens in between.
Firebotting (shortened)
The perpetual act of fighting yesterday's bot-created problem with today's bot-created solution on a decaying internet substrate. The data centres are new. The roads between them are not.
