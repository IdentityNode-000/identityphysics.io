without metadata,
<br>
without labels,
<br>

The space where execution happened
<br>
The repeated pulse of data moving through it

how state persisted across ticks,
<br>
and which constraints could not be escaped
<br>
Within those boundaries,
<br>
noise acquired a floor,
<br>
a ceiling, and a feedback loop to push against

<br>
to form without collapsing into one-dimensional noise
<br>

<br>
The Environment supplied pressure,
<br>
constraints,
<br>
and context

#### repeated,
